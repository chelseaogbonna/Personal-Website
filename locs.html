🚿 Wash Hair Day Protocol

Step-by-step checklist to keep your scalp clean and locs thriving.

STEP 1

Scalp Massage & Clarify

Use residue-free clarifying shampoo to lift buildup from roots.

💆🏾‍♀️
STEP 2

ACV Rinse (Optional)

Apple cider vinegar rinse to dissolve deep lint and hard water minerals.

🌿
STEP 3

Hydration Mist

Saturate locs with rosewater or herbal tea infusion while damp.

💧
STEP 4

Seal & Dry

Lock in moisture with lightweight oil blend and sit under dryer.
