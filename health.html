🗓️ Cycle Phase Blueprint (Gym & Nutrition Alignment)

1. Menstrual Phase (Days 1–5) Low Estrogen & Progesterone

Gym Strategy: Low-intensity steady state (LISS), walking, mat Pilates, gentle mobility. Focus on recovery.

Nutrition Focus: Iron-rich foods (red meat, spinach, lentils), warm soups, berries, magnesium-rich dark chocolate.

2. Follicular Phase (Days 6–12) Rising Estrogen

Gym Strategy: Energy spikes! Great time to try new workouts, progressive overload lifting, and moderate-to-high resistance.

Nutrition Focus: Lean proteins, fermented foods (kimchi, yogurt), fresh vegetables, complex carbs to sustain rising stamina.

3. Ovulatory Phase (Days 13–16) Peak Estrogen & Testosterone Spike

Gym Strategy: Peak strength potential! Best window for personal records (PRs), HIIT, and high-intensity lifting.

Nutrition Focus: High fiber (cruciferous veggies), antioxidant-rich fruits, lighter grains to support liver metabolism of peak estrogen.

4. Luteal Phase (Days 17–28) Rising Progesterone & Metabolic Rate

Gym Strategy: Taper down load. Shift from heavy weights to moderate hypertrophy or light cardio as PMS approaches.

Nutrition Focus: Metabolic rate increases slightly (+100-300 kcal/day). Prioritize complex carbs (sweet potatoes) to curb sugar cravings.
